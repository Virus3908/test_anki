Bundled kanji metadata

Source snapshot: https://kanjiapi.dev/kanjiapi_full.zip
Snapshot date: 2026-09-09
Project source: https://github.com/onlyskin/kanjiapi.dev

This application bundles all 13,108 kanji records as a reduced offline extract
containing the character, English meanings, on/kun readings, school grade,
legacy JLPT level, and Joyo / Jinmeiyo membership. Word and reading indexes
from the full download are not included in this extract.

The underlying dictionary data comes from KANJIDIC2, maintained by the
Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG). See:
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
